# Service business

The service business of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]]: [[alpcare]], the planned [[alpcare-plus]] and the [[remote-monitoring]] behind them. Context, not a sourced fact: linked to the owner's topics "increase of service business sales" and "service backbone" (per CLAUDE.md, section 2). Strategic direction: [[service-first-strategy]].

- Service revenue (AlpCare) is growing more slowly than planned (as of 12 March 2026) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Customers ask for a guaranteed response time under 4 hours (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Remote monitoring reduces on-site visits by about 30% (as of 12 March 2026) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Service share of total revenue: "about one third" (as of 5 May 2026); proposed goal: 50% by 2028 (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Proposed initiatives:
  - [[alpcare-plus]] (CHF 1,900 per robot and month, as of 5 May 2026)
  - [[alpmind-platform]]
  - [[agentic-ai-service-pilot]]

  (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Risks:
  - Price pressure from cheaper pure maintenance contracts
  - Service technicians are hard to find

  (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
