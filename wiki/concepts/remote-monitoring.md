# Remote monitoring

Monitoring of robots in the field via [[alpmind]], as part of [[alpcare]].

- Reduces on-site visits by about 30% (as of 12 March 2026) (reported by [[jonas-weber]]) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Remote monitoring around the clock is part of the proposed [[alpcare-plus]] (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Expected to make up for the shortage of service technicians (as of 5 May 2026) (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

> [!note] Uncertain
> The 30% is an approximate figure ("about"); the minutes give no baseline or measurement period.
