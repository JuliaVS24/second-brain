# Service-first strategy 2026–2028

Strategic direction proposed by [[lea-brunner]] in May 2026: [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] moves "from robot manufacturer to operator partner" (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]). See also [[service-business]].

- Rationale: customers buy the assurance that their warehouse keeps running. The hardware market is getting tighter, with three new providers from Germany and Poland in Switzerland since 2025 (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Goal: service revenue **50% of total revenue by 2028**, up from "about one third" today (as of 5 May 2026) (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Initiatives:
  1. [[alpcare-plus]]
  2. [[alpmind-platform]]
  3. [[agentic-ai-service-pilot]]

  (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Risks:
  - Loss of focus from [[alppick-2-0-launch]]
  - Price pressure from cheaper maintenance contracts
  - AI liability
  - Shortage of service technicians, to be offset by [[remote-monitoring]]

  (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
