# AlpCare Plus

Planned variant of [[alpcare]] with a guaranteed response time under 4 hours (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]). Part of the [[service-business]]. It is initiative 1 of the [[service-first-strategy]] (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]).

## Status (as of 12 March 2026)

- Customers ask for a variant with a guaranteed response time under 4 hours (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- The proposal is to be presented in May together with the strategy memo (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])

## Status (as of 5 May 2026)

- Proposed scope:
  - Guaranteed response time under 4 hours
  - 24/7 [[remote-monitoring]]
  - Replacement robot within 24 hours

  (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Target customers: pharma and hospitals, where downtime is expensive (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Price calculated at **CHF 1,900 per robot and month**; AlpCare stays at CHF 1,200 (as of 5 May 2026) (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Responsible: [[jonas-weber]] (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Decisions

- 12 March 2026: [[jonas-weber]] and [[marco-steiner]] develop a pricing model by the May meeting (7 May 2026, P-02) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- 7 May 2026: the pricing model was to be submitted for approval (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]). The outcome is not in the wiki.
