# Agentic AI in service (pilot)

Initiative 3 of the [[service-first-strategy]]. An AI agent classifies service tickets automatically, resolves standard cases directly and hands complex cases over to humans (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]). Part of the [[service-business]].

## Status (as of 5 May 2026)

- Goal: response time under 10 minutes in the standard case (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Pilot from August 2026 (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Responsible: [[jonas-weber]] with [[priya-raman]] (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Requested pilot budget: **CHF 180,000** (as of 5 May 2026), to be approved at the meeting of 7 May 2026 (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Risks

- Liability: if the agent makes a wrong commitment in service, Alpstein is liable. The memo calls for clear rules on what the agent may do (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])

## Open points

- The rules for what the agent may do are not yet defined in the wiki.
