# Bergland downtime Hall 3 (service incident, June 2026)

Repeated downtime of [[alppick]] robots at [[bergland-logistik-ag]], and the open questions on compensation and the AlpPick 2.0 launch date (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). Relevant to the owner's topic "better customer relations" (per CLAUDE.md, section 2) and to the [[service-business]].

## Status (as of 18 June 2026)

- 15 June 2026: four AlpPick robots in Hall 3 stood still for almost two hours. It was the third incident in four weeks (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- [[remote-monitoring]] reported the error, but nobody was reachable before 10 a.m. (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Cost to Bergland according to [[thomas-ruegg]]: about CHF 4,000 per hour of downtime (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Cause according to [[jonas-weber]]: an error in the navigation software after the update of 20 May 2026. The patch was in testing, with rollout planned for the following week (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- [[sandra-koller]] calls Bergland "our largest customer" (16 June 2026) (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

## Decisions

- None recorded. On 18 June 2026 Weber asked Koller to clarify the way forward with [[marco-steiner]] that day (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

## Open points

- Credit note: Bergland expects one by the end of June 2026. Koller warns that a credit note sets a precedent. Not decided (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- Patch rollout: no confirmation in the wiki.
- AlpPick 2.0 timing: Bergland needs September; Weber assumes Q4 (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). The Board decided on September on 12 March 2026; see the contradiction on [[alppick-2-0-launch]].
- Weber offered AlpCare with a guaranteed response time at CHF 1,450 per robot and month (16 June 2026). According to Koller, pricing had not been decided as far as she knew (16 June 2026). See [[alpcare]] (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

> [!warning] Contradiction
> Rüegg takes Weber's email as a commitment to compensation; Weber says he did not commit to anything concrete (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). Not resolved.

> [!note] Uncertain
