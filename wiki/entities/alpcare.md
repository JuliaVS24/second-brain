# AlpCare

Service subscription of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] (per CLAUDE.md, section 2). Reported on by [[jonas-weber]] at the meeting of 12 March 2026 (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]). Part of the [[service-business]].

- Price: **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 12 March 2026) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- The strategy memo proposes that AlpCare stays at **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 5 May 2026) (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Service revenue is growing more slowly than planned (as of 12 March 2026, no figure given) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Uses [[remote-monitoring]] via [[alpmind]] (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Planned variant with a guaranteed response time: [[alpcare-plus]] (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Price pressure: customers compare AlpCare with pure maintenance contracts, which are significantly cheaper (as of 5 May 2026) (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- [[jonas-weber]] to [[bergland-logistik-ag]] (16 June 2026): "We now offer AlpCare with a guaranteed response time. AlpCare now costs **CHF 1,450 per robot and month**." (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
- [[sandra-koller]] (16 June 2026): the pricing question for AlpCare/AlpCare Plus "has not been decided yet" as far as she knows (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])

> [!warning] Contradiction
> AlpCare price: **CHF 1,200** (12 March 2026, [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]; memo of 5 May 2026 proposes it stays at 1,200, [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]]) vs. **CHF 1,450** with a guaranteed response time (Weber, 16 June 2026, [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]]). The memo puts the guaranteed response time in [[alpcare-plus]] at CHF 1,900, not in AlpCare. Koller says pricing is undecided. Not resolved.
