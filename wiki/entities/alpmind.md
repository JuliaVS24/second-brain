# AlpMind

Fleet software of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] (per CLAUDE.md, section 2), used for [[remote-monitoring]] (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]).

- Remote monitoring via AlpMind reduces on-site visits by about 30% (as of 12 March 2026) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Discussed under the [[alpcare]] service item (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- The strategy memo names AlpMind and AlpCare as Alpstein's edge, not the robot itself (as of 5 May 2026) (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Planned: control of robots from other manufacturers, see [[alpmind-platform]] (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
