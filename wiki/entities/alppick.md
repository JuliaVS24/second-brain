# AlpPick

Picking robot of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]].

- Driver of the +12% order intake in January–February 2026, among logistics customers (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Predecessor of AlpPick 2.0: payload 15 kg (as of 12 March 2026) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Next generation AlpPick 2.0: payload 25 kg, navigation without floor markings (as of 12 March 2026); see [[alppick-2-0-launch]] (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Customer with pilot robots: [[bergland-logistik-ag]] (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Four AlpPick robots at [[bergland-logistik-ag]] stood still on 15 June 2026, the third incident in four weeks. According to [[jonas-weber]], the cause is an error in the navigation software after the update of 20 May 2026; a patch was in testing on 16 June 2026. See [[bergland-downtime-hall-3]] (Source: [[2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland]])
