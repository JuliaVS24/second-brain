# Alpstein Robotics AG

Company with the picking robot [[alppick]], the service [[alpcare]] and [[alpmind]] (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]). The owner's own organization; AlpCare = service subscription, AlpMind = fleet software (per CLAUDE.md, section 2).

- Sites: Appenzell and Buchs SG (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Employees: **120** (as of 12 March 2026) (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Employees: **140** (as of 5 May 2026) (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Order intake January–February 2026: +12% vs. previous year, driven by AlpPick among logistics customers (Source: [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]])
- Service revenue is "about one third" of total revenue (as of 5 May 2026); the proposed goal is 50% by 2028, see [[service-first-strategy]] (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
- Market: three new providers of picking robots from Germany and Poland entered Switzerland in 2025 (Source: [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]])
