# Email thread Bergland Logistik – downtime Hall 3 and AlpPick 2.0, 16–18 June 2026

Raw file: `raw/alpstein/2026-06-18-email-thread-bergland.md`
Export from the mailbox service@alpstein-robotics.example, 18 June 2026. Five emails, 16–18 June 2026.

## Summary

[[thomas-ruegg]] of [[bergland-logistik-ag]] complains that four [[alppick]] robots in Hall 3 stood still for almost two hours. It was the third incident in four weeks, and nobody was reachable before 10 a.m. although [[remote-monitoring]] had reported the error. [[jonas-weber]] names a navigation software error after the 20 May update as the cause, and says a patch would be rolled out the following week. He also offers AlpCare with a guaranteed response time at CHF 1,450 per robot and month, says the AlpPick 2.0 launch is currently assumed for Q4, and writes that "we can do something" about the downtime. [[sandra-koller]] warns him not to commit before [[marco-steiner]] has seen it, because a credit note sets a precedent and AlpCare/AlpCare Plus pricing is undecided. Rüegg then treats the reply as a commitment to compensation and expects a credit note by the end of the month; Weber tells Koller he did not commit to anything concrete. See [[bergland-downtime-hall-3]].

## Key points

- 15 June 2026: four AlpPick robots in Hall 3 stood still for almost two hours; third incident in four weeks (Source: Rüegg, 16 June 2026).
- Remote monitoring reported the error, but nobody at Alpstein was reachable before 10 a.m. (Source: Rüegg, 16 June 2026).
- Cost to Bergland: about **CHF 4,000 per hour of downtime** in extra costs (Bergland's own figure, 16 June 2026).
- Cause: error in the navigation software after the update of 20 May 2026. The patch is in testing and was planned for rollout the following week (Source: Weber, 16 June 2026).
- Weber: "We now offer AlpCare with a guaranteed response time. AlpCare now costs **CHF 1,450 per robot and month**." (16 June 2026)
- Weber: AlpPick 2.0 launch "we currently assume Q4", not yet confirmed (16 June 2026).
- Weber on the downtime: "I think we can do something about that. I will see what is possible." (16 June 2026)
- Koller to Weber: do not commit before Marco has seen this; "Bergland is our largest customer, but a credit note sets a precedent"; the pricing question for AlpCare/AlpCare Plus "has not been decided yet" as far as she knows (16 June 2026).
- Rüegg: "thank you for the commitment". He has told his team that Alpstein will compensate them and asks for the credit note by the end of the month (17 June 2026).
- Rüegg: Q4 is a problem for Bergland, which planned its expansion for September (17 June 2026).
- Weber to Koller: "I did not commit to anything concrete." He asks to clarify with Marco the same day (18 June 2026).

## Open points

- Credit note: Bergland expects one by the end of June 2026; internally nothing has been decided. The outcome is not in the wiki.
- Patch rollout: planned for the week after 16 June 2026; whether it happened is not in the wiki.
- AlpPick 2.0 launch date: September (decision of 12 March) or Q4 (Weber, 16 June)?
- AlpCare price and scope: see the contradiction below.

> [!warning] Contradiction – AlpCare price
> Weber (16 June 2026): AlpCare with a guaranteed response time costs **CHF 1,450 per robot and month**. The [[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]] give **CHF 1,200** (12 March 2026), and the [[2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first]] proposes that AlpCare stays at CHF 1,200, with AlpCare Plus (guaranteed response time) at **CHF 1,900** (5 May 2026). Koller writes on the same day that pricing has not been decided. Not resolved.

> [!warning] Contradiction – AlpPick 2.0 launch
> Executive Board decision of 12 March 2026: launch in **September 2026** ([[2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes]]). Weber on 16 June 2026: "we currently assume **Q4**", not confirmed. No source in the wiki records a decision to postpone. Not resolved.

> [!warning] Contradiction – commitment to compensation
> Rüegg (17 June) understands Weber's email as a commitment to compensation ("thank you for the commitment"). Weber (18 June) says he "did not commit to anything concrete". His wording on 16 June was "I think we can do something about that. I will see what is possible." Not resolved.
