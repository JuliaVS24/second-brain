# Executive Board minutes, 12 March 2026

Raw file: `raw/alpstein/2026-03-12-executive-board-minutes.md`
Meeting: 12 March 2026, 08:30–10:15, Appenzell, Säntis meeting room. Chair: [[lea-brunner]].

## Summary

The Executive Board of [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] reviewed business performance for January–February: order intake was 12% above the previous year, driven by [[alppick]], while [[alpcare]] service revenue grew more slowly than planned. It decided to launch [[alppick-2-0-launch|AlpPick 2.0]] in September 2026; two pilot robots have been running at [[bergland-logistik-ag]] since February. On service, [[remote-monitoring]] via [[alpmind]] reduces on-site visits by about 30%, and customers ask for a guaranteed response time under 4 hours, which led to the [[alpcare-plus]] proposal. Recruitment and a [[digital-onboarding]] process were also discussed.

## Key points

- Order intake January–February 2026: +12% vs. previous year; driver: AlpPick among logistics customers.
- [[alpcare]] service revenue grows more slowly than planned (no figure given). See [[service-business]].
- Headcount: **120 employees** at Appenzell and Buchs SG (as of 12 March 2026).
- **Decision:** no adjustment to the annual budget; next review in June.
- AlpPick 2.0: payload 25 kg (instead of 15 kg), navigation without floor markings.
- **Decision:** market launch of AlpPick 2.0 in **September 2026**.
- Open points AlpPick 2.0: battery life in the cold store (target 8 h, currently 6.5 h); CE certification in progress.
- AlpMind remote monitoring reduces on-site visits by about 30%.
- Customers ask for a guaranteed response time under 4 hours → proposal "AlpCare Plus" to be presented in May with the strategy memo.
- AlpCare price: **CHF 1,200 per robot and month** (as of 12 March 2026).
- **Decision:** [[jonas-weber]] and [[marco-steiner]] develop a pricing model for AlpCare Plus by the May meeting.
- Recruitment in software development: six positions open, four of them in Buchs.
- Onboarding process to be digitized by summer.
- Other business: team event in September, organized by the Executive Board assistant. Next Executive Board meeting: 7 May 2026.
