# Strategy memo "Service first" 2026–2028, 5 May 2026

Raw file: `raw/alpstein/2026-05-05-strategy-memo-service-first.md`
From: [[lea-brunner]] (CEO). To: Executive Board, Board of Directors. Status: draft for discussion at the Executive Board meeting of 7 May 2026.

## Summary

The memo argues that customers buy the assurance that their warehouse keeps running, so [[alpstein-robotics-ag]] should move from robot manufacturer to operator partner (see [[service-first-strategy]]). The goal is for service revenue to make up 50% of total revenue by 2028, compared with about one third today. It proposes three initiatives: [[alpcare-plus]] at CHF 1,900, [[alpmind-platform|AlpMind as a platform]] for mixed fleets, and an [[agentic-ai-service-pilot|AI agent in service]] from August 2026. It names four risks: loss of focus, price pressure, liability for AI, and personnel. The Executive Board is asked to approve the target, the AlpCare Plus pricing model and a pilot budget of CHF 180,000.

## Key points

- Market: the hardware market for picking robots is getting tighter; three new providers from Germany and Poland entered Switzerland in 2025.
- The memo sees the edge in operation, not the robot: [[alpmind]] and [[alpcare]].
- Headcount: **140 employees** (as of 5 May 2026).
- Goal: service revenue **50% of total revenue by 2028**; today "about one third" (as of 5 May 2026).
- Initiative 1, [[alpcare-plus]]: guaranteed response time under 4 hours, 24/7 [[remote-monitoring]], replacement robot within 24 hours. Target customers: pharma and hospitals. Price calculated at **CHF 1,900 per robot and month**. [[alpcare]] stays at **CHF 1,200 per robot and month**. Responsible: [[jonas-weber]].
- Initiative 2, [[alpmind-platform]]: AlpMind should also control robots from other manufacturers. The first interface is for a Bavarian competitor's vehicles at [[rheintal-pharma-ag]]. Responsible: [[priya-raman]].
- Initiative 3, [[agentic-ai-service-pilot]]: an AI agent classifies service tickets, resolves standard cases and hands complex cases to humans. Target response time in the standard case: under 10 minutes. Pilot from August 2026. Responsible: [[jonas-weber]] with [[priya-raman]].
- Risks:
  - Loss of focus if [[alppick-2-0-launch|AlpPick 2.0]] arrives in the fall.
  - Price pressure from cheaper pure maintenance contracts.
  - Liability for wrong commitments by the AI agent; clear rules are needed for what the agent may do.
  - Service technicians are hard to find; remote monitoring must make up for the shortage.

## Decisions needed (Executive Board meeting, 7 May 2026)

1. Approval of the target of 50% service revenue by 2028.
2. Approval of the pricing model for AlpCare Plus.
3. Approval of the pilot budget for Agentic AI (**CHF 180,000**).
